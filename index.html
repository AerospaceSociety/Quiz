Modules

    Proctoring & zero-tolerance policy

    1. Three-strike enforcement. On strike 3 the paper is sealed and submitted immediately, and flagged for panel review.
    2. Immediate strikes: switching tabs or windows, leaving fullscreen for more than 10 s, a second face in view, a frozen or virtual camera feed, or opening developer tools.
    3. Advisories (logged, amber): looking away from the screen or leaving the camera frame. (Microphone audio is not monitored; only camera feed and face positioning are tracked.) Every 3 advisories of one kind become a strike.
    4. Authoritative clock. Time is synchronised with the examination server. Changing the system clock, sleeping the laptop or pausing the page seals the paper.
    5. Lockdown. Copy, paste, printing, right-click, reload and browser shortcuts are disabled and attempts are logged.
    6. One sitting. Your identity is locked to this device until you submit. A reload resumes your session and is recorded.

    Scratch paper is allowed — keep your face visible in frame.
